# Cinematic Lesson Review

## Creative call

Built the selected **Brass Place-Value Machine** storyline: the Wan footage supplies Mira and the workshop, while editable HyperFrames layers own every numeral, label, arrow, and mathematical state. The unbuilt alternatives are **The Number Elevator** and **Tenfold Shadow Theatre**.

## What was checked

- HyperFrames check: zero lint, runtime, layout, or contrast findings.
- Ten authored timeline snapshots: setup, lever, digit travel, empty ones column, zero insertion, equation reveal, and final hold.
- Source QA: verifies 14-second contiguous timing, exact lesson copy, reveal order, local assets, and mounted narration.
- Encoded delivery: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 14.000 seconds, H.264 video, stereo AAC audio, no clipping, −16.2 LUFS integrated.
- Decoded visual frames: the same 2 and 3 tiles move together one column left; zero appears afterward; `230` is absent before the resolve scene.

Audio was measured but not judged by human listening in this environment. The narration uses the local Tara system voice; it is clear and synchronized, but it is the weakest production element compared with a directed neural or human performance.
