# Multiplying Whole Numbers by 10 — Storylines

## Recommendation: The Place-Value Exchange Machine

**Choose this because:** one persistent H–T–O machine can carry the lesson from concrete quantities to the place-value chart to equations without changing metaphors.

Mira receives 23 energy units as two bundles of ten and three single units. Before she pulls the ×10 lever, the learner predicts what will happen; the machine then exchanges each ten for a hundred and each one for a ten, before the same values compress into a place-value chart and finally `23 × 10 = 230`.

- **Device:** a real exchange machine whose chambers are the place-value columns.
- **Form:** one workshop journey with nine chapters; every camera move follows the quantities.
- **Opening:** two ten-rods and three unit cubes sit inside a brass intake tray marked `23`; the unanswered equation `23 × 10 = ?` is already visible.
- **Beat chain:** hook → goal → build 23 → predict → prove 23 → repair “just add zero” → guided 41 → independent 47 → retrieval recap.
- **Look:** child-friendly brass-and-enamel workshop, large manipulatives, persistent H–T–O coordinates, warm daylight, restrained mechanical motion.
- **Sound:** warm narrator, tactile wooden/brass exchanges, quiet rhythmic score, silence during thinking windows.
- **Selected:** the user requested a production-ready plan without a selection stop.

## Alternative: The Parcel Depot

Twenty-three small parcels arrive as two crates of ten and three single boxes. A ×10 order makes every original unit worth ten times as much: crates become pallets of one hundred and singles become crates of ten; shipping bays labeled H, T, and O then reveal 230.

- **Device:** parcel exchange and sorting.
- **Form:** object-to-object chain across a working depot.
- **Opening:** a scanner reads `20 + 3` over two crates and three boxes.
- **Beat chain:** inventory → predict the ×10 order → exchange → sort → misconception inspection → guided shipment 41 → learner shipment 47 → manifest recap.
- **Look:** bright miniature logistics set, yellow safety markings, teal conveyor, cardboard and wood.
- **Sound:** scanner ticks, conveyor rhythm, crate thuds, concise narration.

## Alternative: The Tenfold Garden

Two rows of ten bulbs and three single bulbs form 23. A tenfold growth rule turns each row of ten into a hundred-flower bed and each single bulb into a row of ten; a top-down garden grid becomes the H–T–O chart.

- **Device:** growth by ten with arrays and grouped beds.
- **Form:** overhead match cuts from concrete garden objects to pictorial arrays and symbols.
- **Opening:** the camera counts two ten-bulb strips and three singles into `23`.
- **Beat chain:** plant 23 → predict → tenfold growth → regroup → repair zero shortcut → guided 41 → independent 47 → aerial recap.
- **Look:** crafted stop-motion garden, warm soil, green ceramic markers, cream counting tiles.
- **Sound:** soil taps, leaf rustle, wooden count clicks, light acoustic score.

## Shared assumptions

- Audience: ages 7–10.
- Runtime: 4 minutes 40 seconds.
- Output: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 16:9.
- Pedagogy: concrete → pictorial → symbolic, with prediction, worked reasoning, fading guidance, independent transfer, and recap.
- Scope: multiplying whole numbers by 10 only. Decimal multiplication is named only to reject the unsafe “append a zero” rule; it is not taught here.
- No paid media is required. All mathematical objects and text are deterministic graphics.
