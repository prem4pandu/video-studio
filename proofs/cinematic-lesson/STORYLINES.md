# The Place-Value Shift — Storylines

## Recommendation: The Brass Place-Value Machine

**Choose this:** it makes the operation physically causal—pulling ×10 moves the same two digits into columns worth ten times as much, and the zero appears only after the ones place is visibly empty.

**Device and form:** the object is the explanation; one continuous camera move through a cinematic brass workshop.

Mira presents 23 as two tens and three ones inside a working H–T–O machine. She pulls a ×10 lever; the 2 and 3 move together one column left, then a zero tile clicks into the empty ones place before the camera settles on `23 × 10 = 230`.

**First two seconds:** frame 0 is already alive: Mira's hand places the final third one into the glowing ones chamber while the large digit display reads `23`; the camera is close enough to read both the counters and the T/O columns.

**Beats:** 23 is built → ×10 lever is pulled → both digits shift left one place → zero occupies the empty ones place → 230 is read by place value → equation concludes.

**Look:** deep-teal child-friendly workshop; aged satin brass, clear glass chambers, warm amber working light; bold geometric numerals integrated into the machine.

**Sound:** concise warm narration; tactile tile clacks, weighted lever thunk, one rising mechanical run, soft zero click, resolved marimba-and-strings cadence.

**Selected for `SPEC.md`.** The user requested no stops, so this recommendation is locked without a selection pause.

## Alternative: The Number Elevator

**Device and form:** a place-value elevator; one frame continuously rebuilds.

The digits 2 and 3 are passengers standing on the tens and ones floors of a tall cutaway tower. Mira selects ×10, the entire pair rides left/up to hundreds and tens, and an empty ones platform receives a zero; the architecture makes relative position unmistakable, but the vertical metaphor is less direct than the horizontal classroom chart.

**First two seconds:** the camera looks through the tower facade as 2 checks into T and 3 checks into O; `23` illuminates above the doors.

**Beats:** digits check in → ×10 elevator starts → pair moves one place → empty O is identified → zero arrives → 230.

**Look:** teal-and-copper Art Deco lift, cream enamel column labels, warm practical bulbs, shallow theatrical depth.

**Sound:** lift bell, chain-and-pulley rise, two footstep clacks, quiet spoken explanation, single resolved bell.

## Alternative: Tenfold Shadow Theatre

**Device and form:** multiplication revealed through scale; match cuts between physical groups and projected place-value silhouettes.

Mira sets down two bundles of ten rods and three unit cubes, then turns a ×10 lantern crank. Their projected values transform by match cut into two hundreds and three tens; a circular shutter marks the now-empty ones position and resolves into zero, producing 230 in a more poetic but less literal film.

**First two seconds:** two ten-rods and three unit cubes strike the brass table in rhythm while their shadows form `23` behind Mira.

**Beats:** concrete 23 → crank ×10 → values enlarge tenfold → shadows align H/T → zero closes O → equation.

**Look:** warm amber shadow stage inside the workshop, tactile wood-and-brass manipulatives, deep teal backdrop, hard-edged silhouettes.

**Sound:** block knocks establish rhythm, hand-crank ratchet, paper-shadow sweep, narration, low string harmonic resolving to a wooden chime.
